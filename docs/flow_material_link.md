# One lattice description for flow and its environment

The pole example separates the moving fluid from the material it moves through.
The pole is a persistent environmental feature, not a fresh random kick. The next
connection is to let that feature be the coarse expression of persistent material
states in the finer lattice. This would couple environmental variation and
structure to the fluid response; it is not yet demonstrated by the fixed pole.

Real-valued coarse states do not require real-valued microscopic bits. If B
binary material sites are grouped into one coarse cell, their occupied fraction
s=(sum b)/B takes values 0, 1/B, ..., 1. Directional particle counts averaged over
sites or ensembles similarly supply populations f_a. A cell then has fluid
populations f_a and a material state s. These are physically distinct quantities;
coarse averaging does not by itself provide their dynamics.

A simple coupling lets s determine the fraction of incident populations reflected
rather than transmitted. The existing hybrid bed model already uses a partial
bounce-back rule of this form, together with erosion and transport. It therefore
contains a candidate coupling, but its continuous bed variables have not been
derived from a verified finer binary material model. Its older persistence claims
also need resource accounting and independent checks.

A constructive test would start with a finite irregular material configuration,
then let the lower-layer dynamics determine whether it persists, moves or decays.
The fluid must read that evolving configuration, with no separately pinned pole
mask. Compare against the same material amount dispersed spatially, and a control
where material cannot influence the fluid. Measure feature lifetime and wake
circulation separately. Any erosion or transport must conserve the specified
material apart from explicit boundary exchanges.

This tests possibility without estimating a rare origin rate. It does not require
all noise configurations to produce a feature, or require indefinite persistence.
At present the validated pole experiment establishes the fluid response to a
supplied persistent feature. The lower-layer origin and persistence of that
feature remain the next missing connection.
