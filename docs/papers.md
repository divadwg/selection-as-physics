# Papers and research directions

The current [two-paper map](../paper/README.md) separates the exact sorting
prediction from the broader route to inherited growth. [Scope and publication
gaps](two_paper_scope.md) state their current claims.

Paper 1 includes uniform microscopic activity, a specified flow-dependent
interaction law and the exact conditional concentration window. A
[deterministic-map pilot](hidden_map_results/README.md) begins testing the missing
microscopic-to-stationary connection. The [memristor assay audit](memristor_q_analysis.md)
reopens a specific prospective experiment without claiming that read-noise
exponents or aggregate nanowire measurements validate the theorem.

Paper 2 retains separate spatial and inheritance constructions and the
[copying-clock prediction](copy_clock_results/README.md). Its common physical
construction remains open. The drafts are not peer-reviewed publications.
