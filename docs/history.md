## 9. ΛB post-mortem

Original ΛB (2026 model, rejected at preprint): survival defined by throughput, so measuring that survival tracks throughput was near-tautological; heredity installed by hand; mutation a flat rate with no observer; dissipation-invariance result was algebraic (Price covariance with no loss rates). What survived: dissipation is the price, not the selector (consistent with T1 vs T9); parasitic persistence in network architecture under anti-coupling was the one non-predictable result (an early sighting of capture, T5). New "self-routing ratio" is a counterfactual, not a covariance; not the same object.


## 7. Ladders and distinctions kept from the conversation

- Crystal / whirlpool / organism: no flux; flux but passive; flux procured by the pattern's own activity. A continuum measured by D6, no vitalism.
- One-shot vs installment payment of order: same laws, different decay rates. Life is the case where creation never finishes.
- Two instabilities: depletion-driven (bottle chugging: flow destroys its enabling gradient through a shared channel; fix is channel separation: vent, gut, vortex core) and shear-driven (tap: boundary friction, above threshold rolls up). Conjecture, held loosely: a loop is what a flow does with a mismatch it cannot shed.
- Persistence / growth / partition / heredity / unlimited heredity (Maynard Smith). Physics gives the first three free (a river splits and captures); portable state (seed) is where heredity as such begins; unlimited heredity needs combinatorial capacity (P8).
- Loops in flow and loops in memory: one theorem twice.
